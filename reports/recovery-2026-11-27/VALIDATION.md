## Authorized batch execution

Reserve a window clear of the routine scan. Use a fresh, separately authorized
read-only export for every exact health key from the generated query. The runner
checks the export when the batch starts and before each source reservation.

```sh
python scripts/verify_recovery_board_batch.py \
  --approve-and-run "$PING_APPROVED_PLAN_SHA256" \
  --health-export "$PING_READ_ONLY_HEALTH_CSV"
```

first send. The checkpoint under `output/recovery-board-batch-427` binds to
the approved revision and plan. Resumes retain completed and interrupted

approval can resume deferred, untouched sources after a refreshed health
snapshot when their provider is eligible.
